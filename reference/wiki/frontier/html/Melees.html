Within the freedom space of frontier, you may encounter many different types of weapons. The ones listed here are much better suited to bashing, bonking and stabbing, than shooting. Ranging from your own fists to illegal and ridiculous weaponry, there's no shortage of different bludgeons and blades within the frontier.


There are 5 main categories of weapons, swords, knives, axes, spears, and bludgeons. This page is dedicated to covering all the different melee weapons in frontier.
