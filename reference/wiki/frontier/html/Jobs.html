Contractors[edit]

Contractor, pilot and mercenary are the most common jobs. These jobs do not come with prescribed responsibilities or gameplay expectations.

The jobs are functionally similar: make your own choices, get hired or buy a ship. Make money, or spend it. Space is big and you are free to pursue your own interests.

Contractors are do not have a base salary, and instead earn money by getting hired by other players or by other means, such as selling salvage materials or artifacts. You should expect to earn spesos through your own actions, either providing services or collecting materials or treasures to sell to other players or to the trade mall. You are free to pursue any path, be it salvage, mining, science, mercenary combat, food service, and much more.

Fhe shipyard console at the Frontier Outpost (as well as other locations) offers a wide selection of ships suited for different tasks. You are also free to modify any ship to fit your intended experience. Specialized shipyard consoles on the expeditionary lodge, medical dispatch (as well as other locations) can give you access to more specialized ships for healthcare and mercenary combat. Please note that these tend to be more expensive, and are generally more suitable once you have enough experience and money saved.

Starting on a Point Of Interest (POI)[edit]

Contractor is the first free job you will have access to. contractors start their shift only on the medbay of the Frontier Outpost. If you are planning your first shift, you will find more information in the Getting Started guide.

Pilot and Mercenary have the same (lack of) responsibilities as the contractor. They have access to different loadout gear, and the mercenary may purchase combat firearms at the expeditionary lodge. Otherwise they are also free to pursue their own interests instead of performing prescribed tasks.

Pilot and mercenary may also start their shifs on the EL and MD, which grants access to specialized ships.

Starting on an existing ship[edit]

In the lobby, while selecting your starting location, you will be able to select "ship" jobs.

Ship jobs are positions opened by the captain of an existing ship looking to hire additional crew members. You are not required to use this system to be hired (you may ask in round, on the radio or in person), but prospective employers will need to come pick you up on location. If you join through the ship menu, you will appear directly on their vessel instead as a candidate hologram.

As a candidate, you may discuss jobs details, payroll and other terms of employment. Both the hiring capatain and yourself need to accept the agreement (through the matching action) in order for you to join the ship. In that case, you will be instantly teleported on location. If either of you choose to refuse the interview instead, you will be sent back to the lobby without penalties.

Sector staff[edit]

These roles represent employees of the sector. In contrast to contractors, these roles have well defined functions, and it is expected that you fulfill them at the best of your ability.

Note that there is no automatic payroll. the various station finances - and payroll -are managed by command staff.

If you join while no command staff is available, you can wait until one becomes available or ask on Ahelp for assistance with salary, but there is no guarantee that your time worked will be honored if no one is available to do so. If you join while a command staff for your department is available, consider discussing terms of employment with them, such as base salary, bonuses for performance, or other relevant details.

Staff ships: most staff jobs will be provided with a ship coupon in their loadout when starting a shift. The ship coupon may be used to obtain a free ship, suitable for their responsibilities. To redeem this free ship, use the coupon as you would use an ID on the relevant shipyard console (not the public shipyard console). Remmember to keep the coupon, as it will hold the deed for the ship, and is used to return the ship (sell it) at the end of your shift. Note that selling the ship voids the coupon.

Note: command roles are allowed to fulfill payroll for other departments in cases when command for that department is not currently staffed or unavailable. For example, the sheriff may pay service staff if no SR is present.

Medical[edit]

command: director of care

The medical department does not have joinable medical jobs from the lobby, but is instead operated by independent contractors. The director of care is encouraged to work with, and establish compensation and contracts for medical responders, but this is done in-round through character interactions, not through an enforced job system.

Law Enforcement[edit]

command: sheriff

The NFSD is the sector's Sheriff department, in charge of law enforcement for the area. For more information, please consult the NFSD Jobs page, and the NFSD Standard Operating Procedure page.

Non-job roles[edit]

Several roles do not act as "jobs". This includes ghost roles, borgs and prisoners.

These roles do not have expectations of making money, most lack a bank account, and should be understood as purely roleplay roles.
