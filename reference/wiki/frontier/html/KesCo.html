KesCo are proud ship builders who have over the years brought to the Nanotrasen fleet, both civil and military, a fine catalog of vessels for various uses across the New Frontiers program. They trace their origins to the Sol System with their headquarters based on Luna, as well as a Dry Dock in orbit around Luna and Frontier Prime.

KesCo, commercial spacecraft builders was founded in 2149. KesCo is privately owned by a State Research and Production Company, which holds 49 percent.

KesCo Ship Catalogue[edit]

This is the catalogue of ships from KesCo available on Frontier.

While originally focused on larger ship designs, KesCo has taken on various contracts, buildling luxury cruisers, military vessels, and cargo haulers alike.
