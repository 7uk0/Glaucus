Medical Overview[edit]

The ultimate goal of medical care is to save lives. Life in the frontier is a hazardous one, and a lost life can result in a life's savings if medical rescue does not arrive at all.

Standard medical operations consist of a select few roles: Doctor, Chemist, and Botanist. These roles are not exclusive, and a single person may perform the duties of all three.

Frontier Station's medical operations expand upon the standard in roleplay roles such as Leadership, Search and Rescue, and Combat Rescue.

Standard Medical Operations[edit]

Typical casualty care is handled with medicinal chemicals by doctors and interns depending on the damage values listed by a Health Analyzer or a Medical PDA. Chemists provide and maintain a ready supply of medicine for use by doctors, and are also supplied by botanists with medicinal plants such as aloe and galaxythistle for more specific and highly-sought-after chemicals that cannot be synthesized.

Types of Damage[edit]

Brute - Physical trauma inflicted upon the victim. Brute damage is categorized as either Blunt, Piercing, or Slashing wounds. Brute damage will slowly recover over time if the patient is under 20 total damage. Blunt damage can be recovered by sleeping in a bed.

Burn - Tissue and nerve damage inflicted by excessive Heat, frostbite(Cold), electrical Shock, or chemical dissolution(Caustic). Heat damage will recover over time if the patient is below 20 total damage. Defibrillators always inflict 5 Shock damage per use.

Airloss - Hypoxic damage from a lack of oxygen due to Asphyxiation or Bloodloss. Asphyxiation is caused by either a lack of air in the environment or suit air tank or gasping for air while in critical condition. Bloodloss damage begins below 80% of a patient's maximum blood capacity. Airloss damage will slowly recover on over time if the patient is supplied with air and sufficient blood.

Toxin - Bodily contamination causing illness or disease due to the presence of Poisons or Radiation. Poison can be recovered by sleeping in a bed, but radiation damage requires proper medical treatment.

Genetic - Cancerous damage to a victim's Cellular structure. Genetic damage requires proper medical treatment to cure.

Rot - A victim that has succumbed to their wounds begins a decomposition timer upon death. A corpse can be resuscitated normally with a defibrillator as long as the body is not rotting. Rot occurs 20 minutes after death, and can be cured through cryopod treatment.

Doctor[edit]

The forefront of the medical center, Doctors practice medicine on the dead and dying with the use of medical resources available and in the most resource-efficient manner possible. Health Analyzer in hand, syringe in the other, the only condition a well-trained and well-supplied doctor cannot save a patient from is gibbing, or a bodily explosion resulting from excessive blunt force trauma or rot decomposition.

Topical Treatments[edit]

Topical treatments do not require extensive medical knowledge to understand their purpose, but are slow to apply, typically weak in potency, and generally cheap to manufacture in a Medical Techfab.

Bruise Pack - Treats 5 points of damage in each of the three types of Brute damage.

Ointment - Treats 5 points of damage from Heat, Cold, and Shock. Treats 1.5 points of damage from Caustic.

Roll of Gauze - Primarily used to stop bloodloss, but can be used to treat some Brute damage. Rolls of cloth may be used in emergencies.

Blood Pack - Adds 5% blood to a patient and treats some bloodloss damage. Bloodloss damage in a live patient stops above 80% blood level.

Aloe Cream - Created by cooking aloe, works as an alternative to Ointment.

Medicated Suture - An advanced topical created in a Medical Assembler, treats 10 points of each Brute damage as well as stopping bleeding.

Regenerative Mesh - An advanced topical created in a Medical Assembler, treats 10 points of each Burn damage including Caustic.

Chemical Treatments[edit]

The primary form of treating injuries in a live patient, doctors will administer medicinal chemicals most often due to the high potency and speed of recovery provided. It is important to note that standard chemicals require a patient to be able to metabolize it; therefore, a dead casualty cannot process chemicals and must be treated to a revive-able damage level with topical treatments or cryopod treatment.

The range of medicinal chemicals is very extensive and is far too large to list in a general guide, but a short list of basic general-purpose chemicals will allow for the general populace and medical trainees to identify and administer proper care in emergency situations.

Bicaridine - A general purpose low-potency Brute chemical that treats 6.6 points of damage in all three categories per 5u dose. Overdoses at 15u and above will cause asphyxiation and poison damage with a chance of vomiting at over 30u. Side effects include drunkenness.

Kelotane - A general purpose very low-potency Burn chemical that treats 3.3 points of Heat, Shock, and Cold damage per 5u dose. Often used as an ingredient for more advanced burn medicine and almost never used as a treatment except for in emergencies. Overdoses at 30u causes the patient to become dehydrated.

Dermaline - A general purpose medium-potency Burn chemical that is a direct upgrade to Kelotane and treats 10 points of Heat, Shock, and Cold damage per 5u dose. Overdoses at 10u and above causes the patient to suffer asphyxiation, hypothermia, and mild physical trauma.

Dexalin - A medium-potency Airloss chemical that treats 10 points of Asphyxiation damage and 5 points of Bloodloss damage per 5u dose. Overdoses at 20u and above causes greater asphyxiation to the patient and hypothermia. Often further processed into Dexalin+ for use as a treatment.

Dexalin Plus - A high-potency Airloss chemical that is a direct upgrade to Dexalin and treats 35 points of Asphyxiation damage and 30 points of Bloodloss damage per 5u dose as well as counteracting Heartbreaker Toxin. Overdoses at 25u and above causes greater asphyxiation and hypothermia.

Dylovene - A medium-potency anti-Toxin chemical that treats 10 points of Poison damage per 5u dose. Overdoses at 20u and above cause physical trauma to the patient and can induce vomiting. Side effects at and above 15u include drunkenness.

Hyronalin - A medium-potency anti-Toxin chemical that treats 10 points of Radiation damage per 5u dose. Side effects include vomiting. Overdoses at 30u and above causes the patient to suffer Heat damage. Often used as an ingredient for more advanced medicines and almost never used as a treatment.

Arithrazine - A high-potency anti-Toxin chemical that is a direct upgrade to Hyronalin and treats 30 points of Radiation damage per 5u dose. This chemical is overdose-safe, however, side effects include 15 points of physical trauma and causes the patient to bleed during treatment per 5u dose. Often mixed with Bicaridine to counteract side effects, though use of Gauze to stop bleeding is sufficient for a mostly healthy patient.

Inaprovaline - A slow-acting emergency-use life-saving chemical that prevents a patient in critical condition from asphyxiating to death. Treats 120 points of Asphyxiation damage and reduces blood loss to a casualty in critical condition over 50 seconds per 5u dose. Often used as an ingredient to more advanced chemicals and almost never used as a treatment in favor of Epinephrine. Usage with Dylovene converts both into Tricordrazine.

Epinephrine - A rapid-acting emergency-use life-saving chemical that quickly stabilizes a patient in critical condition. Treats 30 points of Asphyxiation damage and minor recovery to Brute and Burn injuries per 5u dose. Overdose at 20u and above causes the patient to suffer asphyxiation and poison damage. 

Emergency Medipen - A standard issue life-saving auto-injector given to all personnel containing 12u Epinephrine and 3u Tranexamic Acid as a blood clotter. Allows rapid first-aid to casualties while awaiting the arrival of medical support.

Chemist[edit]

Most ships will have a chemist room, with their basic chemist stuff such as chemvendor, chem-dispenser, chem-master aswell as a little kit with chemist goggles to inspect the chemicals a beaker holds, and so on. And most likely it is next to the botany room, where you can grow you own little medicinal plants so you can make certain medicine or assemble some stuff.

However it isn't very often to be "taken" since anyone can really just do chemicals as long as they know what to do, however, in bigger ships such as Crescent it might be worth it having atleast a person dedicated to chemist to make sure the big ship who will have a lot of people roaming has enough chemicals to treat them all.

But in practice, yeah the same from doctor can apply here.

Botanist[edit]

It is no secret some medical ships will have a little garden section, usually with 3-4 hydro plates for your medicine plants (or food), usually you want to plant aloe, poppy, ambrosia and galaxythistle, as they can be used for assembling regenerative mesh and medicated suture, or also for their chemicals to make certain medicine, such as Aloxadone requiring grinded Aloe.

Notes: These plants can still be affected by mutagen so they can have certain mutations, (and they might give extra chemicals, however in my case i didn't get much chances so i MIGHT be wrong), however usually you're just fine with using the base form, since they're easier to maintain and harvest.

If you wish more information for botanist, you can look in the rest of the pages and also on the official wiki of SS14

Apothecary[edit]

Apothecary image

The Apothecary is a small ship, which uses uranium as fuel (either sheets or fuel rods, just like the rest), which is perfectly fine to solo use it, maybe you can have a buddy to help you with chemicals or botany but that's it.

It is a ship more designed into supplying other ships with medical stuff, either chemicals or assembled medipens.


Benefits:

- It's small and easy to use, it's a nice choice if you're new to a chemist type ship.

- You start with all medicinal plant seeds and with a seed extractor, which is perfect to supply yourself with these nice plants!

- It costs just 36k spesos, being an easy ship to get with a fresh new character.


Things to keep in mind:

- Lack of cryogenics room, making it difficult to revive some "NPCS" from the emergency drops found in wreckages (as usually they will have 200+ dmg of a type and also airloss damage)

- Due to being small it can be slightly difficult/annoying to modify it, such as adding some scavenger machines to be self-sufficient

- Despite having a botany section, it does not have a biogenerator, making it difficult to get food without a grub ship, or to recover important seeds if you miscalculate (it's not really that bad but remember it)

- Lacks a chemvendor, so the chemvendor restocks are useless to you until you manage to build one, which might give some trouble to cook up chemicals in the mean time.


General tips: This is mostly my experience, however, as I stated, this ship is more designed to deliver medical supplies to other people, either other medical ships or sell/trade them with other workers (or even give them for free!), your focus should be creating chemicals and also assembling certain medical items, to know the recipes just look in the cookbook (make sure is in frontier fork) and use the "Medicinal" filter.

Even if you're unable to save someone, remember, you're not alone! radio it through medic channel and let others know you got someone who needs wakey wakey but you don't have the means for it.

It is important to also scavenge in the wreckages, so you can make more medical supplies, since you want to go there for the emergency drops anyways.

And also, something worthy to mention, usually mid round someone will build a cryogenics room in MD, if you're nearby and notice someone doing it, help them by preparing cryogenics medicine, you'll save up some headaches for them, and also while you're at it and if you got extra chemicals, give them some more chemicals to share with others, this way you save up having to fly to other medical ships in case they need a refill.

Druid[edit]

It is a medium ship, considered to be a Botanist Class, being evident the ammount of hydroplates that it has compared to other ships that are not botanist. Due to the structure and machines it starts with, it is a ship designed to be a medical supplier (and even basic ingredients for cooking since you got the biogenerator), it's a great ship to keep contact with, as it can supply your crew with those very useful items, specially if you plan to do expeditions.

It uses uranium as power, but also has solar panels with a solar power computer, where you must control the panels and try to make sure they turn towards the sun constantly (with the turning º each min), do note that when moving it might mess up your previous setup, so it is a good habit to check it usually to make sure your generator doesn't need to make a lot of power, this way you can save up uranium, and do not move if it's not needed.

Recomended crew: 2-3, it is true it's perfectly fine to solo it, however it can be kinda stressful having to pay attention to the radio in case someone needs supplies, then tending to your 2 gardens and also make chemicals, if you're atleast 2 it can greatly boost the ship's efficency.

Machines avaiable from second 0:

- 2 Chem-masters

- A chem-dispenser

- A biogenerator

- A hotplate

- A fridge

- A blender

- Medical Techfab

- Service Techfab

- Medical assembler

- Scrapper (in the power room)

- 16 hydroplates

- A seed extractor

- A crate for your seeds and crops

General ideas

As always, you can play your ship as you wish, however this section is to give little ideas about what shenanigance you can do (feel free to contribute more!)


1- Supply the MD with some medical stuff (medipens, cryogenic meds, medical sutures, regenerative mesh, etc) and make it clear that everyone should share.


2- Always pay attention to what chemicals your plants might give, aloe vera can give dermaline, poppies can give bicaridine, linghzi can give epinephrine and ultravasculine chemicals (perfect for the poison medipens) and so on, it is true it's not as effective as making them yourself, however it is an "infinite" supply of those, it's just time consuming. (Extra: if you feel you got balls of steel, use lots of mutagens to randomize the chemicals the plants might give, naturally it's not always guaranteed to add a chemical, it might be other stuff, so keep in mind or.. ignore it if you wish)


3- Thanks to your scrapper and blender you can get some of the base elements, some more annoying than others, always check the recipe book for it, you might find a little treat.


4- You should scavenge, even trash (not scrap sadly) it's useful for you, you can get lots of materials this way to build your medical supplies and being always ready, aswell as seeds! the thing you will want mostly, since your biogenerator cannot do all seeds, such as the spaceman's trumpet.


5- If you really want to give some use to your scrap, try to get a scrap reclaimer, it will be a great way to get some blueprints for free aswell as materials


6- Announce yourself, since you got way more hydroplates, you can make plants way quicker, and thanks to your biogenerator it's even easier, let all people know you're ready to supply workers and other medical ships with precious medical supplies, as they're useless if they sit by you, you're quite slow and you don't have cryogenics room at all. (of course you can keep some just in case, since you might wanna scavenge from time to time)
