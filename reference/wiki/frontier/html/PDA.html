PDA: Personal Data Assistant, is the smartphone equivalent in Space Station. Equipped with various slots to hold different useful tools, and a EasyClip™ Jumpsuit clip for use on the go. The user can also install various programs to aid their Journey into Frontier space. Coming in various types, there is surely a PDA right for you.
